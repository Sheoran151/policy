# Privacy Policy — Color Sort Puzzle

**Package ID:** `com.coloursort.game`  
**Effective Date:** November 27, 2026  
**Contact Email:** sheoransandeep151@gmail.com  

This Privacy Policy explains how **Color Sort Puzzle** handles information when you use our Android mobile application.

## 1. Offline-First Gameplay & Local Storage
Color Sort Puzzle works completely offline without requiring user accounts, logins, or a backend server. Your unlocked levels, stars, daily challenge completions, hint balance, current puzzle state, and settings are stored strictly locally on your device via Android DataStore.

## 2. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)
To keep the game free to play, the app integrates **Google AdMob** and **Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP)** for Banner, Interstitial, and optional Rewarded Ads.

- Google AdMob may process device and usage data (such as Android Advertising ID, IP address for coarse location/fraud prevention, device details, and ad interaction metrics) in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google Advertising Policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
- Where required by law (e.g., EEA/UK GDPR), consent is requested through Google UMP prior to personalized ads, and non-personalized ads are used otherwise.

## 3. Permissions
- `INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Used solely by Google AdMob / UMP to fetch ads when online.
- `VIBRATE`: Used for optional tactile haptic feedback (toggleable in Settings).

## 4. Data Deletion
No gameplay data is stored on our servers. You can delete all local progress anytime via **Settings → Reset Progress** in the app or by uninstalling the app.

## 5. Children's Privacy
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region).
